May–Aug 2023

REU Research Assistant, Google-Sponsored

Rice University · Houston, Texas

  • Converted 42,401 irregular ocean-bed records into 15,345 analysis-ready nodes across 34 geographic survey blocks, engineering 19 model features and a 104,368-edge spatial graph while eliminating 63.8% of invalid, non-surface, duplicate, or unmatched records.
  • Constructed and evaluated seven spatial graph definitions using 5–20-neighbor kNN and 10–50 km radius connectivity, quantifying mean degree, isolated locations, and geographic coverage across all 34 survey blocks.
  • Reduced spatially held-out grain-size RMSE from 2.245 for a train-mean baseline to 2.097 with graph-augmented gradient boosting, a 6.6% reduction, by incorporating aggregated neighborhood features from a 10-neighbor spatial graph.
